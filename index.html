Dynamite Lake

Two pontoons. Two coolers. One fishing rod with a stick of dynamite on the lure.

Pick your rig, captain

Bigger pontoon = bigger target, but it hauls a lot more beer — and beer cans are your only defense.

How to play

  1. Anchor up. Every round both boats pick a new distance from shore: 25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250 or 300 feet. Your opponent never sees where you moved.
  2. Cast. Guess how far out their pontoon is, then stop the swinging power meter (click or hit space). Stop it dead center and the dynamite lands exactly where you aimed; sloppy timing and it splashes short or long.
  3. Hit window. A hit only counts if the dynamite lands within your target boat's length — big pontoons are far easier to clip than little ones.
  4. Beer-can defense. They take four seconds to wind up, then the dynamite flies at you. Chuck a beer can (click or space) while the lure is inside the red strike zone and it blows up over open water. Throw early or late and you just waste a beer.
  5. Splash talk. After every cast you're told how far short or long you were — but they will have moved by the time you cast again.
  6. Five hits sinks a pontoon. Last cooler floating wins.
