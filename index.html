Dynamite Lake

Two pontoons. Two coolers. One fishing rod with a stick of dynamite on the lure.

Pick your rig, captain

Bigger pontoon = bigger target, but it hauls a lot more beer — and beer cans are your only defense.

How to play

  1. Anchor up. Every round both boats pick a new distance from shore: 50, 100, 200 or 300 feet. Your opponent never sees where you moved.
  2. Cast. Hit CAST (or space) and a marker sweeps left to right along the distance scale — 50, 100, 200, 300 ft. Hit STOP at the range you think they are sitting at; the dynamite lands exactly where the marker stopped.
  3. Hit window. A hit only counts if the dynamite lands within your target boat's length — big pontoons are far easier to clip than little ones.
  4. Beer-can defense. They take four seconds to wind up, then the dynamite flies at you. Chuck a beer can (click or space) while the lure is inside the red strike zone and the dynamite always detonates mid-air, well clear of your deck. Throw early or late and you just waste a beer.
  5. Splash talk. After every cast you're told how far short or long you were — but they will have moved by the time you cast again.
  6. Three hits sinks a pontoon. Every hit leaves craters, holes and smoke, and the boat rides lower — so you can see who is winning from across the lake.
  7. Sound. Honky-tonk techno on the boat radio and two old guys running their mouths after every cast. Kill it with Sound: on/off at the bottom.
